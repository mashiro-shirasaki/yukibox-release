Everything a reference library should do

Built around one idea: your references are yours. No accounts, no upload, no lock-in — just fast local tools that stay out of your way.

Local-first & private

Images and the SQLite database live on your own disk. No account, no cloud, no telemetry — fully usable offline.

Tag everything

Hierarchical tags with categories. Drag images onto a tag to file them — with a confirmation step so nothing lands by accident.

Drag & drop import

Drop in files or entire folders. For every import you choose: copy into the library folder, or link images where they live.

Marquee select & batch ops

Eagle-style box selection across the grid. Tag, move or delete dozens of images in one gesture.

Smart color extraction

Dominant palettes are extracted from every image automatically — spot the right mood at a glance.

Pixel-level viewer

A smooth zoom & pan viewer for checking linework, edges and details up close.

Keyboard-driven

Shortcuts for filtering, tagging and navigation — organize hundreds of images without leaving the keyboard.

Cross-platform

Native builds for Windows 10+, macOS 11+ (Apple Silicon & Intel) and Linux (AppImage).
